作为共犯的法院：巴西青少年司法体系中对警察酷刑的纵容

Courts as accomplices: Enabling police torture in the Brazilian juvenile justice system

Punishment & Society /2026-08-03 /刑事诉讼 /推荐指数 7/10

基于对巴西六个首府185场初始听证和19名少年法官的访谈，揭示法院通过司法沉默、道德置换和官僚化中和技术纵容警察酷刑，表明问题在于制度运作而非失灵。

摘要全译

法官如何回应执法人员对现行犯被抓获的涉法青少年实施的酷刑？尽管此类暴力仍然报告不足，法院也很少果断采取行动，但本文直接考察了治安法官在初次听证会上如何处理这些指控。基于对巴西六个首府城市185场初次听证会的非参与式观察和对少年法院法官的19次半结构化访谈，分析识别出司法系统维持警察酷刑有罪不罚的三种机制：司法沉默，即指控未被记录或在程序上被绕过；道德转移，即注意力从警察行为转向青少年及其家庭的品行和行为；以及官僚化中和，即正式法律语言将暴力报告转化为行政上无用的记录。这三种机制共同揭示出，问题不在于制度失灵，而在于制度运作：法院不仅未能阻止警察滥用权力——它们还通过将暴力吸收进官僚常规而参与其常态化。

原文摘要（openalex）

How do judges respond to torture committed by law enforcement officers against court-involved youth apprehended in flagrante delicto ? Although such violence remains under-reported and courts rarely act decisively, this paper directly examines how magistrates address these allegations during initial hearings. Drawing on non-participant observation of 185 initial hearings and 19 semi-structured interviews with juvenile court judges from six Brazilian capitals, the analysis identifies three mechanisms through which the judiciary sustains impunity for police torture: judicial silence, through which allegations are left unrecorded or procedurally bypassed; moral displacement, through which attention is redirected from police conduct to the character and behaviour of adolescents and their families; and bureaucratic neutralisation, through which formal legal language transforms reports of violence into administratively inert records. Together, these mechanisms reveal that the problem is not one of institutional failure but of institutional functioning: Courts do not merely fail to prevent police abuse – they participate in its normalisation by absorbing it into bureaucratic routine.

Mariana Chies-Santos, Debora Piccirillo, Maria Gorete Marques De Jesus

02

重返：作为一种性别化的监禁连续体——监督、忽视与惩罚的延伸

Reentry as a gendered carceral continuum: Surveillance, neglect, and the extension of punishment

Theoretical Criminology /2026-08-08 /刑罚学 /推荐指数 7/10

基于对葡萄牙女性和假释人员的定性纵向研究，本文揭示重返社会作为一种性别化和空间延伸的惩罚机制，结合监督、制度性忽视与遗弃，再现结构性不平等，而非促进社会再融入，并呼吁将重返重构为反惩罚的集体关怀实践。

摘要全译

重返社会通常被呈现为监禁与社会重新融合之间的桥梁。本文基于一项对在葡萄牙被监禁及假释出狱女性的定性纵向研究，展示了重返社会如何作为一种性别化且空间延展的惩罚机制运作，其特征是 surveillance、制度性忽视与遗弃并存。重返社会并未促成社会重新融合，反而在监狱与社区空间中再生产了结构性不平等、污名化及具身化伤害。通过将分析置于批判性重返研究及女性主义废奴主义学术之中，本文将上述视角延伸至南欧语境，展示了监禁权力如何通过福利缩减、低强度监管以及监禁的持久且往往无形的后果而持续存在。本文呼吁将重返社会重新构想为一种基于结构性转型与集体关怀的反惩罚实践。

原文摘要（openalex）

Reentry is commonly presented as a bridge between incarceration and social reintegration. Drawing on a qualitative longitudinal study with women incarcerated and released on parole in Portugal, this article shows how reentry operates as a gendered and spatially extended regime of punishment, characterized by the coexistence of surveillance, institutional neglect and abandonment. Rather than enabling social reintegration, reentry reproduces structural inequalities, stigma, and embodied harms across prison and community spaces. Situating the analysis within critical reentry studies and feminist abolitionist scholarship, the article extends these perspectives to a Southern European context, showing how carceral power persists through welfare withdrawal, low-intensity supervision, and the enduring, often invisible impacts of incarceration. It calls for rethinking reentry as an anti-punitive practice grounded in structural transformation and collective care.

Silvia Gomes

“任何有意义的东西都只是梦想”：性犯罪者登记与通知对女性伴侣和母亲马斯洛需求层次感知的扰乱

‘Anything meaningful is just a dream’: perceived disruption of Maslow’s Hierarchy of Needs among female partners and mothers affected by sex offender registration and notification

Psychology, Crime & Law /2026-08-04 /刑罚学 /推荐指数 7/10

基于马斯洛需求层次，调查SORN政策对女性伴侣和母亲基本需求、安全、归属、自尊及自我实现的感知扰乱，发现污名、隔离、经济压力及情感困扰，并显示一定韧性。

摘要全译

性犯罪者登记与通知（SORN）政策不仅影响要求登记的个体，也影响其家庭成员，但他们的经历仍未得到充分探索。本研究以马斯洛需求层次理论为指导，考察了女性伴侣（n=53）和母亲（n=44）对SORN政策如何影响其福祉的感知。参与者完成了一项调查，涉及她们对基本需求、安全、爱与归属、自尊以及自我实现的追求。研究结果显示，所有领域均受到干扰，包括连带污名、社会隔离、经济压力、住房不稳定以及对人身安全的担忧。受访者描述了以羞耻、绝望、自责和照护负担为特征的情感困扰。女性伴侣经常表达对自己、子女及伴侣安全的恐惧，而母亲则报告了更为明显的自责，并将已登记成年子女的福祉置于自身福祉之上。尽管面临这些挑战，一些人通过倡导、重新定义和面向未来的应对方式展现了韧性。总体而言，研究结果表明，对马斯洛需求层次理论追求的感知干扰破坏了家庭稳定和社会支持，而这些对重返社会和长期成功至关重要。该研究强调了政策改革和定制化支持服务的必要性，这些服务应认识到家庭在康复、社区安全及预防性犯罪中的关键作用。

原文摘要（openalex）

Sex offender registration and notification (SORN) policies affect not only the individuals required to register but also their families, yet their experiences remain underexplored. Guided by Maslow’s Hierarchy of Needs, this study examined perceptions of how SORN policies influence the wellbeing of female partners (n = 53) and mothers (n = 44). Participants completed a survey that addressed their pursuit of basic needs, safety, love and belonging, self-esteem, and self-actualization. Findings indicated disruptions across all domains, including courtesy stigma, social isolation, financial strain, housing instability, and concerns for physical safety. Respondents described emotional distress marked by shame, hopelessness, self-blame, and caregiving burdens. Female partners frequently expressed fear for their own, their children’s, and their partners’ safety, while mothers reported more pronounced self-blame and prioritized the wellbeing of their registered adult child over their own. Despite these challenges, some demonstrated resilience through advocacy, reframing, and future-oriented coping. Overall, findings suggest a perceived disruption to the pursuit of Maslow’s Hierarchy of Needs which undermines family stability and social supports essential for reintegration and long-term success. The study highlights the need for policy reform and tailored support services that recognize families’ vital role in rehabilitation, community safety, and the prevention of sexual offending.

Line Christophersen, Danielle Arlanda Harris, Jill Levenson

“深呼吸”：阿拉巴马州死囚牢房中的医学伦理与视觉讽刺

“Take Deep Breaths”: Medical Ethics and Visual Satire on Alabama’s Death Row

International Journal of Offender Therapy and Comparative Criminology /2026-08-10 /刑罚学 /推荐指数 6/10

通过死囚凯里·格雷森在氮气处决前的讽刺画作，探讨医疗专业人员参与死刑执行对希波克拉底誓言“不伤害”的违背，突显美国死刑执行的医疗化问题。

摘要全译

本文通过凯里·格雷森（Carey Grayson）于2024年在阿拉巴马州被以氮气处决前创作的视觉艺术，探讨叙事犯罪学。这些艺术以讽刺手法描绘了约瑟夫·安托吉尼（Joseph Antognini）博士——一位作为专家证人、为氮气作为处决方法的安全性作证的麻醉师。当通过叙事序列与重述的视角研究时，格雷森的讽刺肖像提出了关于医疗专业人员日益参与处决的重要伦理问题，这损害了这些专业人员所宣誓的“不伤害”的希波克拉底誓言。格雷森的讽刺以其即将面临的处决为窗口，探索各州在致命注射处决失败后，试图确立符合第八修正案的过程中，对新方法进行试验的荒谬性。我们将这些艺术作品与安托吉尼在格雷森法律挑战中的专家证人证词记录进行三角验证，以凸显美国处决的“医疗化”现象。

原文摘要（openalex）

This paper approaches narrative criminology through the visual art produced by Carey Grayson prior to his execution by nitrogen gas in the state of Alabama in 2024. The art satirically represents Dr. Joseph Antognini, an anesthesiologist who testifies as an expert witness in favor of the safety of nitrogen gas as a method of execution. When studied through the lens of narrative sequencing and restorying, the satirical portraits by Grayson raise important ethical questions about the increasing involvement of medical professionals in executions, which undermines the Hippocratic oath these professionals take to "do no harm." Grayson's satire uses his own imminent execution as a window for exploring the absurdity of states' experimentations with new methods as they attempt to establish compliance with the Eighth Amendment in the wake of botched executions related to lethal injection. We triangulate this art with transcripts of Antognini's expert witness testimony in Grayson's legal challenge to highlight the "medicalization" of executions in the U.S.

Katie Owens-Murphy, Kacey Keeton

使用PanelMatch估计不规则处理模式下警察干预的因果效应：对纽约警察局2014-2019年夏季全出警计划的评估

Using PanelMatch to Estimate the Causal Effects of Police Interventions with Irregular Treatment Patterns: An Assessment of the NYPD’s Summer All Out, 2014–2019

Journal of Quantitative Criminology /2026-08-03 /犯罪学 /推荐指数 6/10

本文使用双重固定效应和新型匹配估计器PanelMatch评估纽约警察局夏季全出警计划对犯罪结果的影响，发现该计划对暴力犯罪和财产犯罪无显著影响，且传统方法的结果偏差源于内生部署。

摘要全译

摘要 目标 纽约警察局的“夏季全员出动”（SAO）是一项反复实施的、以在场为基础的步行巡逻举措，在2014年至2019年间被非随机地部署到各警区。我们同时使用双向固定效应（TWFE）和一种新颖的匹配与加权估计器（PanelMatch），来评估SAO部署是否影响了多种犯罪结果。方法 由于处理模式在时间上间歇性变化，我们使用TWFE（一种双重差分（DiD）方法）分析了为期10年的警区-月份-年份面板数据。随后，我们采用Imai等人（2023）的PanelMatch进行扩展，这是一种在该领域从未使用过的因果推断估计器，它根据预处理犯罪轨迹和警区层面协变量的相似性来选择并重新加权对照单位，从而解决警区的内生性定向以及随时间变化的群体构成问题。结果 TWFE估计表明，SAO与暴力犯罪的增加相关，而财产犯罪则未检测到显著变化。当应用PanelMatch（采用倾向得分匹配和加权，并在细化过程中使用滞后结果和警区协变量）时，这些效应衰减至接近零。我们发现SAO步行部署对暴力犯罪或财产犯罪没有统计学上显著的影响。这些结果在使用不同犯罪类型和替代函数形式的稳健性检验中仍然成立。TWFE与PanelMatch结果之间的差异与SAO警员被派往经历短期犯罪激增的警区这一情形相一致。结论 更传统的TWFE与新颖的PanelMatch分析结果之间的差异表明，对间歇性、重复定向干预的评估应考虑利用预处理结果历史的设定来解决内生性分配问题。从实质上看，SAO基于在场的步行巡逻并未在平均意义上显著减少暴力犯罪或财产犯罪——在大型辖区内部署固定巡逻可能不会产生普遍的威慑效应。

原文摘要（openalex）

Abstract Objectives The New York City Police Department’s Summer All Out (SAO) was a recurring, presence-based foot-patrol initiative non-randomly deployed to precincts between 2014 and 2019. Using both two-way fixed effects (TWFE) and a novel matching and weighting estimator ( PanelMatch ),we assess whether the SAO deployment influenced a variety of crime outcomes. Methods Since the treatment pattern varies over time intermittently, we analyze a 10-year precinct-month-year panel using TWFE, a difference-in-differences (DiD) methodology. We then expand with Imai et al.’s (2023) PanelMatch , a causal inference estimator never before used in this field, which selects and reweights control units based on similarity in pretreatment crime trajectories and precinct level covariates, thereby addressing endogenous targeting of precincts and evolving group composition over time. Results TWFE estimates suggest SAO was associated with increases in violent crime, while property crime shows no detectable change. When applying PanelMatch , which employs propensity-score matching and weighting, and used lagged outcomes and precinct covariates in the refinement process, these effects attenuate toward zero. We find no statistically significant impact of SAO foot deployments on violent or property crime. These results persist with robustness checks using different crime types, including alternative functional forms. The divergence between TWFE and PanelMatch results is consistent with a scenario where SAO officers were assigned to precincts experiencing short-term crime surges. Conclusions The divergence between the results of a more traditional TWFE and the novel PanelMatch analysis suggest that evaluations of episodic, repeatedly targeted interventions should account for endogenous assignment using designs that leverage pretreatment outcome histories. Substantively, SAO’s presence-based foot patrols did not measurably reduce violent or property crime on average—reallocating officers for fixed deployments within large jurisdictions may not yield general deterrent effects.

Thomas J. Bilach, Sean Patrick Roche
